title: Accessibility
description: "How BuzzahBuddy supports text size, VoiceOver and TalkBack, contrast, reduced motion and large buttons."
lead: BuzzahBuddy is built for unsteady hands and tired eyes.
---

- **Text size:** the app follows your phone's text size. Layouts stack at large sizes so nothing is cut off.
- **Screen readers:** the app works with VoiceOver on iOS and TalkBack on Android.
- **Contrast:** turn on **High Contrast** in **Settings**, or use your phone's own contrast setting.
- **Motion:** the app follows your phone's reduced motion setting.
- **Large buttons:** main actions are large and sit near the bottom of the screen, within easy reach.
