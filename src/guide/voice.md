## Siri voice commands (iOS)

You don't need to open the app first. Siri voice commands need iOS 16 or later, and they work in English only.

### Commands

Say "Hey Siri", then one of these phrases. **BlueBuzzah** is the main word to use in every voice command.

You can also say **Glove**, **Gloves** or **BuzzahBuddy** in its place. For example, "Start Gloves session" or "Check BuzzahBuddy battery".

| To do this | Say |
|---|---|
| Start a session on your current profile | "Start BlueBuzzah session" |
| Start a Regular session | "Start BlueBuzzah Regular session" |
| Start a Noisy session | "Start BlueBuzzah Noisy session" |
| Start a Hybrid session | "Start BlueBuzzah Hybrid session" |
| Start a Custom session | "Start BlueBuzzah Custom session" |
| Start a Gentle session | "Start BlueBuzzah Gentle session" |
| Pause your session | "Pause BlueBuzzah session" |
| Resume your session | "Resume BlueBuzzah session" |
| Stop your session | "Stop BlueBuzzah session" |
| Hear each glove's battery level | "Check BlueBuzzah battery" |
| Hear your profile, or your session's state and time left | "Check BlueBuzzah profile" |

### Good to know

- **Stopping asks first.** Siri asks "End your session?" Say yes to stop. If you say no, your session keeps going.
- **Naming another profile restarts your gloves.** Siri says your session starts when the gloves restart. It then starts on its own.
- **Your gloves need to be connected.** Siri doesn't scan for gloves. Keep **Auto-Connect to Last Used Gloves** on, with both gloves turned on and near your phone. If they can't connect in time, Siri says "Your gloves aren't connected."
- **Siri follows the same rules as the app.** It won't start a session if a glove's battery is too low, or if your secondary glove isn't connected. It tells you why.
- **Quick Test has no voice command.** If Quick Test is your current profile, "Start BlueBuzzah session" still starts it.
- **New installs need a moment.** Right after you install BuzzahBuddy, Siri may need a few minutes to learn the phrases.
- **You can use them from the Shortcuts app too.** BuzzahBuddy adds Start Session, Pause Session, Resume Session, Stop Session, Check Battery and Check Profile to the Shortcuts app.

## Voice commands on Android

Voice commands aren't available on Android yet. Google and Gemini don't officially support this kind of app command on Android yet. It's on our roadmap. For now, you can do everything with the buttons in the app.
