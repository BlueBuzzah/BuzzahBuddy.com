## Start a session

1. Turn on both gloves and connect to them.
2. Put on your gloves.
3. On the **Session** tab, check that the card at the top says your gloves are connected.
4. Check the profile shown on the **Therapy Profile** card.
5. Tap **Start Session**.

The ring in the middle of the screen shows how much time is left. Your phone stays still during a session. The gloves are the only thing that buzzes.

## Pause, resume or stop

- **Pause** holds the session. The button then changes to **Resume**. Paused time isn't counted in your history.
- **Stop** asks you to confirm. Tap **Stop Session** to end it, or **Keep Going** to carry on. The time so far is saved.

<!-- planned-image: /guide/img/11-stop-confirmation | screenshot | Shows: The "Stop This Session?" sheet over the Session tab. Arrow: Points to the Stop Session button. -->

## When a session ends

BuzzahBuddy saves your session and asks if you want to add a note. Write anything you want to remember, then tap **Save Note**, or tap **Skip**. Sessions under 1 minute aren't saved.

<!-- planned-image: /guide/img/12-add-a-note | screenshot | Shows: The "Add a Note?" prompt after a session, with the note field and the Save Note and Skip buttons. Arrow: None. -->

## If you disconnect or lose the connection

If you disconnect, your phone stops controlling the gloves, but a running session keeps going on the gloves. If the connection drops during a session, BuzzahBuddy tries to reconnect and shows a banner on the **Session** tab. Keep your phone near your primary glove.
