## See when an update is ready

BuzzahBuddy checks online for new firmware, and that's the only thing it uses the internet for. When an update is ready, the **Gloves** tab shows it:

- An **Outdated** badge appears next to the firmware version.
- A **Firmware Update Available** banner appears at the top of the tab.

## Update your gloves

You can't update firmware from your phone. Use the BlueBuzzah Updater on a computer:

1. Tap **How to Update** on the banner to see the steps.
2. Tap **Share Updater Link** to send the link to your computer.
3. On a computer, download the [BlueBuzzah Updater](https://bluebuzzah.com/docs/updater/) from BlueBuzzah.com.
4. Connect your primary glove with its USB cable and follow the steps in the Updater.
5. Do the same for your secondary glove.

Always update both gloves. They need to run the same firmware.
