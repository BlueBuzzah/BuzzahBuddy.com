---

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Auto-Connect to Last Used Gloves** | Connects to the last gloves you used when BuzzahBuddy opens |
| **Theme** | Follows your phone's setting, or stays Light or Dark |
| **High Contrast** | Uses stronger colors and edges |
| **Demo Mode** | Uses pretend gloves, so you can try BuzzahBuddy without real ones |
| **Clear All Data** | Deletes your saved sessions and notes and forgets your last glove and profile |
| **About** | Shows information about the app, and the 2 notes from the Welcome screen |

Turning on **Demo Mode** disconnects your gloves. While it's on, **History** shows 60 days of sample sessions, and sessions you run aren't saved. Your own history comes back when you turn Demo Mode off.

**Clear All Data** can't be undone.
