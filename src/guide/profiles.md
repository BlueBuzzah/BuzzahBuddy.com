## The 6 profiles

| Profile | Session length | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Regular** | 2 hours | Steady timing. Each glove uses its own random finger order. |
| **Noisy** | 2 hours | Slightly varied timing. Both gloves use the same finger order. |
| **Hybrid** | 2 hours | Slightly varied timing. Each glove uses its own finger order. |
| **Gentle** | 1 hour | Softer buzzes in a fixed finger order. |
| **Quick Test** | 5 minutes | A short session to check that your gloves work. |
| **Custom** | You choose | Your own settings. The only profile you can edit. |

## Change your profile

1. Open the **Gloves** tab.
2. Under **Session Profile**, tap the profile you want.
3. Tap **Apply Settings**.
4. Tap **Apply and Restart**.

Your gloves restart to use the new profile. They usually reconnect on their own in under a minute. Keep both gloves on and near your phone while you wait.

You can't change the profile during a session. Stop the session first.

## Make your own Custom profile

1. On the **Gloves** tab, tap **Custom**.
2. Tap **Edit Settings**.
3. Change the settings you want.
4. Tap **Save**.
5. Tap **Apply Settings** to send them to your gloves.

| Setting | Range |
|---|---|
| **Buzz Length** | 50 to 200ms |
| **Pause between Buzzes** | 30 to 200ms |
| **Session Length** | 1 to 240 minutes |
| **Softest Buzz** | 20 to 100% |
| **Strongest Buzz** | 20 to 100% |
| **Timing Variation** | 0 to 50% |
| **Same Pattern on Both Gloves** | On or off |

If your settings differ from the published study, BuzzahBuddy shows a note at the top of the screen. They're still within what the gloves accept, but no study has tested them. Tap **Use Study Settings** to fill in the study's values.
