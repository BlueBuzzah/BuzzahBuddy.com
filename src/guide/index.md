## What you need

- **2 BlueBuzzah gloves.** One is set up as your primary glove and the other as your secondary glove. You set this once with the [BlueBuzzah Updater](https://bluebuzzah.com/docs/updater/) on a computer.
- **A phone or tablet.** BuzzahBuddy runs on iOS 15 or later and Android 7.0 or later. [Get the app](/#download).
- **Bluetooth turned on.** BuzzahBuddy asks for permission to use it the first time you scan. Older Android versions may also ask for location access, which they need for Bluetooth scanning.
- **Charged gloves.** We recommend charging both gloves before a session.

Your phone connects to your primary glove. Your secondary glove connects to your primary glove. You need both gloves on to start a session.

## The app at a glance

The tab bar at the bottom of the screen has 4 tabs.

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Session** | See your gloves' status, and start, pause and stop sessions |
| **Gloves** | Connect, pick a therapy profile, change glove settings and test motors |
| **History** | See your past sessions, add notes and share a report |
| **Settings** | Change auto-connect, appearance, Demo Mode and data options |
