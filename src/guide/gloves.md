## Test your motors

Use **Test Motors** to check that each finger motor buzzes. You can't test motors during a session.

1. On the **Gloves** tab, tap **Test Motors**.
2. Pick a **Strength**. Start low, and go higher if you can't feel it.
3. Pick a **Buzz Length**. Longer buzzes are easier to notice.
4. Tap each finger to make it buzz. A check shows that finger is done.

## Other glove settings

- **Turn Off the Glove Light during Sessions** keeps the status light dark so it won't distract you. It's under **Device Options** on the **Gloves** tab. Tap **Apply Settings** to send the change to your gloves.
- **Refresh Device Info** reads the latest details from your gloves.
- **Disconnect** ends the connection between your phone and your gloves.

Glove settings are locked while a session is running or paused.
