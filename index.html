About BuzzahBuddy

  • iPhone and Android

    Available in both smartphone ecosystems with full feature parity.

  • Wireless control

    Connects to your gloves over Bluetooth. Control therapy sessions and customize settings without a cable.

  • Session history

    Track and report on therapy session history; duration, profiles, and parameters.

Important

People with implanted electrodes from deep brain stimulation (DBS), heart pacemakers, or other internal electronic devices should take special precautions around all electronic devices, including BlueBuzzah. Consult your physician before use.
